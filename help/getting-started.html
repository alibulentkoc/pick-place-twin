Getting started

Both twins are single HTML files. They run in any modern browser, need no install and work offline.

Open a twin

Pick the twin that matches what you are doing.

The two twins
TwinUse it forFile

Amiga watermelon twinA battery-powered harvester on a farm-ng Amiga rover that drives along a rowtwins/pick-place-twin-amiga.html

Open the file directly from your computer, or use the links in the left rail. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work. A laptop with a separate graphics chip runs the leaf canopy scenes more smoothly.

Find your way around the screen

  • 3D view (left): the rig, the objects and the camera frame on the floor.
  • Live equations (top left): the numbers behind the motion. Use Show and Hide to open or close it. The tabs are Mechanics, Camera and Messages.
  • OAK camera view (bottom left): what the depth camera sees, with detection boxes.
  • Panel (right): controls, live hydraulic circuit, electronics, readouts, results and settings.

A first run in five minutes

  1. Open the Amiga watermelon twin.
  2. In step 1, set How many to 4, keep Leaves on None, and press Plant.
  3. In step 2, keep In the deck bin, then press Start: drive along the row and pick.
  4. Watch the status line under the toolbar. Set Speed to 4x if it feels slow.
  5. When the row is finished, open Results to see picks, cycle time and energy per pick.

The twins are teaching and design tools. They model the main physics and the camera pipeline, but they are not a substitute for testing real hardware.
