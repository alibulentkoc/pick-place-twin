Getting started

Each twin is one HTML file. It operates in all modern browsers. It needs no installation and it operates offline.

Open a twin

Select the twin for your task.

Open the file from your computer, or use the links in the left column. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari operate correctly. The leaf canopy scenes of the Amiga twin move more smoothly on a computer with a graphics card.

The screen

  • 3D view (left): the rig, the objects and the camera frame on the floor.
  • Live equations (top left): the calculations behind the movement, with the current numbers. Push Hide or Show to close or open the box. The tabs are Mechanics, Camera and Messages.
  • OAK camera view (bottom left): the image of the depth camera, with the detection boxes.
  • Panel (right): the toolbar, the numbered steps, the live hydraulic circuit, the electronics, the readouts, the results and the settings. A step with the Required label is necessary for the design project. A step with the Optional label is not necessary.

Design project: check a design in ten minutes

  1. Open the lab and tractor twin.
  2. Step 1: push Load tractor start.
  3. Step 2: push Open my design settings. Enter your values in the five folders with the Required label: Arm geometry, Cylinders and hydraulics, Load, Bin and pick width, and Counterbalance valves.
  4. Step 3: push Show worst point. The tip moves to the worst point of the full work path.
  5. Read Readouts. Make sure that Pick width reached shows yes and that Rim clearance over the inner rim shows OK. Record the two cylinder forces and pressures at the worst point.

The Lab and tractor twin page gives the details.

Amiga twin: a first run in five minutes

  1. Open the Amiga Hydraulic Arm twin.
  2. Step 1: set How many to 4. Then push Plant.
  3. Step 2: select In the deck bin. Then push Start: drive along the row and pick.
  4. Read the status line under the toolbar. If the movement is too slow, set Speed to 4x.
  5. At the end of the row, open Results. Read the picks, the cycle time and the energy for each pick.

The twins are teaching and design tools. They model the main physics and the camera pipeline. They do not replace tests on real hardware.
