Settings reference

The function of each setting, folder by folder, in the sequence of the panel.

The settings are at the bottom of the panel. In the lab and tractor twin, the five folders with the Required label contain your design values for the design project. All other folders are optional. The two twins use the same names for the folders that they share.

Lab and tractor twin

Arm geometry (in): required

Arm geometry (in): required settings
SettingFunction
Pivot spacingThe distance between the two cylinder pivots on the bar.
Bar heightThe height of the pivots above the ground.
Retracted lengthThe pin-to-pin length of each cylinder when it is fully retracted.
StrokeThe cylinder stroke. The extended length is the retracted length plus the stroke.
Gripper lengthThe grip length: the vertical distance from the tip to the center of the fruit.

Cylinders and hydraulics: required

Cylinders and hydraulics: required settings
SettingFunction
Bore (in)The piston diameter. It sets the piston area, the push force and the extension speed.
Rod (in)The rod diameter. It sets the annulus area and the retraction speed.
Pressure (psi)The relief valve setting.
Flow setting (gpm)The pump or flow control setting.
Actual flow error (%)The difference between the real flow rate and the setting.

Load: required

Load: required settings
SettingFunction
ShapeBall (round fruit) or Bale.
Height (in)The height of the object. For a round fruit, this is the diameter.
Weight (lb)The fruit weight.
Gripper weight (lb)The end effector weight. The twin adds it to the load at the tip.

Bin and pick width: required

A marker shows the drop point: green if the rim clearance check passes, red if not.

Bin and pick width: required settings
SettingFunction
Show the binShows the bin. It also turns on the drop point, the rim clearance check and the work-path search.
Pick width (in)The width across the row where fruit can lie, centered on the arm. Orange marks on the ground show its two ends.
Bin rim height (in)The height of the top edge of the bin above the ground.
Bin inner rim distance (in)The horizontal distance from the arm center to the bin wall nearer the arm.
Bin width (in)The inside width of the bin. The drop point is at its middle.
Rim clearance (in)The minimum gap between the bottom of the fruit and the rim.

Counterbalance valves: required

Counterbalance valves: required settings
SettingFunction
Fitted on both cylindersInstalls a counterbalance valve on the port that holds the load on each cylinder.
Setting / highest load pressureThe setting as a ratio of the highest holding pressure in the work path. The twin uses this ratio when the setting in psi is 0.
Setting (psi), 0 = use ratioThe valve setting in psi. Use it for a valve with a minimum setting, for example 435 psi.
Pilot ratio (x:1)The ratio of the pilot area to the load area, for example 4:1.
Valve leakage at 1000 psi (in3/min)The leakage through the valve. It causes slow drift when the arm holds a load.

Gripper details: optional

Gripper details: optional settings
SettingFunction
Actuator stroke (in)Sets the widest jaw opening.
Closed width (in)The gap when the jaws are fully closed.
Actuator force (lb)The squeeze force. Each jaw gets half.
Actuator speed (in/s)The jaw closing speed.
Pad friction (mu)The grip friction. With the force, it sets the heaviest fruit that the jaws can hold.

Camera mount: optional

Camera mount: optional settings
SettingFunction
Post height (in), Arm back (in)The position of the camera.
Aim automatically, Aim at pick line (else above)The twin aims the camera.
Tilt from vertical, Field of viewManual aim and the lens angle.

Camera pipeline: optional

Camera pipeline: optional settings
SettingFunction
Detection noise (px), Disparity noise (px)Image noise and depth noise.
Stereo baseline (in)The distance between the two stereo lenses.
Detection image (px)The image resolution for detection.

Controller: optional

Controller: optional settings
SettingFunction
Deadband (in)The position error at which the controller stops a move.
Camera delay (ms), Camera rate (Hz)The delay and the rate of the camera feedback.
Noise at 40 in (in)The noise of the tip marker position.
Descent near objects, Step per cylinder (in)Stepped or direct descent, and the step length.

View: optional

View: optional settings
SettingFunction
Workspace map, Map showsThe shaded reach region, and what its color shows (force for each pound of load, or lift capacity).
Camera view cone, Camera frame on floorCamera overlays.
Tip trailThe path of the tip.

Amiga watermelon twin: additional folders

The Amiga twin has Arm geometry, Cylinders and hydraulics, Camera mount, Camera pipeline, Controller and View, as above. It has no Bin and pick width folder and no Counterbalance valves folder. It also has these folders:

Rover (farm-ng Amiga)

Rover (farm-ng Amiga) settings
SettingFunction
Track width (in), Wheelbase (in)The distance between the left and right wheels, and between the front and rear axles.
Rover weight (lb)Used in the tip-over check.
Arm plane behind rear axle (in)The distance from the rear axle to the plane in which the arm moves.
Frame height (in)The height of the rover frame and deck.
Drive speed (in/s)The speed between stops.

Watermelon

Watermelon settings
SettingFunction
Shape, Height (in)Round or elongated fruit, and the fruit height (the short diameter).
Elongated: length / heightThe length of elongated fruit.
Weight (lb), Gripper weight (lb)The fruit weight, and the end effector weight added to the tip load.

Gripper (electric actuator)

Gripper (electric actuator) settings
SettingFunction
Wrist rotationInstalls the wrist. The gripper hangs 1.5 in lower.
Wrist motor speed (deg/s)The turning speed of the wrist.
Actuator stroke, Closed width, Actuator force, Actuator speed, Pad frictionAs in Gripper details above.

Transfer conveyor

Transfer conveyor settings
SettingFunction
Handoff height (in)The height of the low end of the extended conveyor.
Belt speed (in/s)The belt speed.
Slide actuator stroke (in), Extend time (s)How far the conveyor slides down to extend, and the time to extend or stow.
Bin wall height (in)The wall height of the deck bin.
Belt motor (W), Extend actuator (W)Electrical loads.

Detect while driving

Detect while driving settings
SettingFunction
Camera search range ahead (in)The farthest distance that the camera searches.
Blind spot near the arm (in)The last distance that the rover drives by odometry.
Mound thresholdThe minimum size of a depth bump that counts as fruit.
Odometry errorThe drift of the distance estimate of the rover.
Leaf piles and clods per 10 ftFalse targets in leafy fields.
Sightings to confirmThe number of detections before the rover stops for a possible fruit.

Upgraded hardware

Upgraded hardware settings
SettingFunction
Max tip speed (in/s)The speed limit of the planned paths.
Tip acceleration (in/s2)How quickly a move increases and decreases speed.
Position gain (1/s)The size of the correction from the cylinder position sensors.
Sensor noise (in)The noise of the string potentiometer readings.

Canopy

Canopy settings
SettingFunction
Leaf cover min and maxThe range of leaf cover on each watermelon.
Strip clearing (air knife)Installs the air knife.
Clearing effectivenessThe part of the leaves that the air knife removes each second in the cleared strip.
Wrist puff radius (in)The area that the puff at the gripper clears.
Depth mound error (in)The position error when the camera finds the fruit only by its depth bump.
Row canopy densityThe leaves for each unit of area in the whole-row canopy.

Power unit and battery

Power unit and battery settings
SettingFunction
Battery voltage (V), Battery energy (Wh)The Amiga battery.
Motor x pump efficiencyThe overall efficiency of the hydraulic power unit.
Motor no-load power (W)The power when the motor operates with no load.
Line and valve losses (psi)The pressure loss between the pump and the cylinders.
Camera + controller (W), Gripper actuator (W)Electrical loads.
Arm weight (lb), Power unit weight (lb)Used in the tip-over check.

View (Amiga twin)

View (Amiga twin) settings
SettingFunction
Camera follows roverThe view moves with the rover. It turns on automatically when a run starts.
