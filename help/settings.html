Settings reference

What each setting does, folder by folder, in the order they appear.

Settings are at the bottom of the panel in the Amiga watermelon twin. The lab and tractor twin uses the same names for the folders it shares.

Rover (farm-ng Amiga)

Rover (farm-ng Amiga) settings
SettingWhat it does
Track width (in)Distance between left and right wheels.
Wheelbase (in)Distance between front and rear axles.
Rover weight (lb)Used in the tip-over check.
Arm plane behind rear axle (in)How far behind the rear axle the arm works.
Frame height (in)Height of the rover frame and deck.
Drive speed (in/s)Speed between stops.

Arm geometry (in)

Arm geometry (in) settings
SettingWhat it does
Pivot spacingDistance between the two cylinder pivots on the bar.
Bar heightHeight of the pivots above the ground.
Retracted lengthPin-to-pin length of each cylinder when fully retracted.
StrokeCylinder stroke.
Gripper lengthDistance from the tip joint to the grasp point.

Cylinders and hydraulics

Cylinders and hydraulics settings
SettingWhat it does
Bore (in)Piston diameter; sets force and extend speed.
Rod (in)Rod diameter; sets the rod-side area and retract speed.
Pressure (psi)Relief setting.
Flow setting (gpm)Pump or flow-control setting.
Actual flow error (%)How far the real flow differs from the setting.

Upgraded hardware

Upgraded hardware settings
SettingWhat it does
Max tip speed (in/s)Speed cap for planned paths.
Tip acceleration (in/s2)How fast moves speed up and slow down.
Position gain (1/s)Correction from the cylinder position sensors.
Sensor noise (in)Noise on the string pot readings.

Power unit and battery

Power unit and battery settings
SettingWhat it does
Battery voltage (V) and energy (Wh)Amiga battery.
Motor x pump efficiencyOverall efficiency of the hydraulic power unit.
Motor no-load power (W)Power drawn while the motor runs with no load.
Line and valve losses (psi)Pressure lost on the way to the cylinders.
Camera + controller (W), Gripper actuator (W)Electrical loads.
Arm weight (lb), Power unit weight (lb)Used in the tip-over check.

Gripper (electric actuator)

Gripper (electric actuator) settings
SettingWhat it does
Actuator stroke (in)Sets the widest jaw opening.
Closed width (in)Gap when fully closed.
Actuator force (lb)Squeeze force; half goes to each jaw.
Wrist motor speed (deg/s)How fast the wrist turns.
Wrist rotationFits the wrist; the gripper hangs 1.5 in lower.
Actuator speed (in/s)Jaw closing speed.
Pad friction (mu)Grip friction; with force, sets how heavy a fruit can be held.

Watermelon settings
SettingWhat it does

Transfer conveyor settings

Canopy

Canopy settings
SettingWhat it does
Leaf cover min and maxRange of cover for leaves over each watermelon.
Strip clearing (air knife)Fits the air knife.
Clearing effectivenessShare of leaves blown away per second in the cleared strip.
Wrist puff radius (in)Area cleared by the puff at the gripper.
Depth mound error (in)Position error when fruit is found only by its depth bump.
Row canopy densityLeaves per area for the whole-row canopy.
