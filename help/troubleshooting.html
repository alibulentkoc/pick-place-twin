Troubleshooting

Frequent questions, and what to check first.

Rim clearance over the inner rim shows NOT OK.
The front of the fruit passes too near the rim. The readout gives the x position and the tip height that is necessary. Increase the bin inner rim distance, or change the geometry (for example, a higher bar or a longer stroke).
Pick width reached shows NO.
The tip cannot reach grasp height at the two ends of the pick width. Lower the bar, increase the stroke or the retracted length, or change the pivot spacing. Change one value at a time and record the effect.
the forces at the worst point are much larger than on the pick line.
This is correct. The worst point is often the drop point above the bin, where the two cylinders are almost parallel. Use the forces at the worst point of the work path for your design.
the load sinks, or the status line shows a counterbalance warning.
The counterbalance setting is less than the load pressure. Increase the setting in psi, or the ratio. If the valves are not fitted, the load drifts slowly through the valve spool. Tick Fitted on both cylinders.
The twin stopped because of an error.
Push Reset rig. If the status line shows an error message, copy it into an issue on the GitHub repository.
