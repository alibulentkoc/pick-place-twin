Troubleshooting

Common questions, and what to check first.

I pressed Start and nothing seems to happen.
Read the status line. While driving it updates every second with the distance to go. The view now follows the rover during a run; if it was off, use Fit view to rig. If watermelons from an earlier planting are still in the field, new ones go beyond them: press Remove all watermelons, then Plant.
The rover went off screen.
Turn on Camera follows rover under Settings, View, or use Fit view to rig. Right drag pans, the wheel zooms.
Many watermelons are skipped.
Check Picks refused by checks in Results. Elongated fruit lying across the row are too wide for the jaws: tick Wrist rotation or raise the actuator stroke. A full deck bin stops the run: press Empty the deck bin. Under heavy leaves with camera finding, some misses are expected.
"Gripper hit the watermelon during the move."
The open jaws would have swept into a fruit. Reload the page so you have the latest version. If it repeats, note the hardware, shape, leaf and finding choices so the case can be reproduced.
I see air blowing with Leaves set to None.
Choosing None removes the leaves only when you press Plant again. Until then the air knife keeps clearing the old leaves.
The wrist does not seem to turn.
It turns only for elongated fruit, or with the Wrist angle slider in Manual control. Round fruit look the same from every side, so the wrist stays at 0 degrees.
The page is slow.
Whole-row leaves at high coverage draw thousands of leaves. Lower the leaf coverage, close the live equations panel, or use a computer with a graphics chip.
Something went wrong and the twin stopped.
Press Reset rig. If the status line shows an error message, copy it into an issue on the GitHub repository.
