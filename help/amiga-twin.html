Amiga Hydraulic Arm twin

A battery-powered hydraulic arm on a farm-ng Amiga rover. The rover drives along a watermelon row. The camera finds each fruit, and the arm puts it in a side tray or on a conveyor. The conveyor fills a bin on the deck.

The panel, from top to bottom

The panel is in the sequence that you use it. The toolbar stays at the top when you scroll.

Sections of the panel
SectionContent
ToolbarE-stop, Pause, Speed (1x to 8x), Reset rig, Reach region on or off, and the status line. The status line tells you what the machine does now.
1 Plant watermelons (Required)Set How many, then push Plant. The fruit goes ahead of the rover, in the reach of the arm. Push Plant again to add more fruit farther ahead. The Options box (optional) sets the leaves (None, Over each watermelon, or Whole row), the shape (Round or Elongated) and the leaf coverage (0 to 100%). Remove all watermelons and Empty the deck bin are below.
Place one watermelon yourselfPuts one fruit at an x and y position that you enter, or at a random position. It also moves the side tray.
2 Pick them (Required)Select where the fruit goes (side tray or deck bin). Then push Start: drive along the row and pick. The Options box (optional) sets leaf clearing, the hardware, the wrist rotation and the camera search while the rover drives. A help line below the choices tells you what will occur.
Pick without drivingPicks one fruit, or all fruit, at the arm. The rover does not move.
3 Manual control (Optional)Hold buttons for each cylinder and for the rover, the gripper, home, Show worst point, a move to a point (with the camera or by timing), a wrist angle slider, and sliders that set the cylinder lengths directly.
Rover setup (Optional)Remove harvest module lifts the full harvest unit (frame, arm, cylinders, camera, power unit, conveyor and bin) onto its stands. The rover can then drive away for other tasks. Install harvest module drives the rover back and locks the unit on again.
Hydraulic circuit (live), Parts, ports and hosesThe live circuit, and the part numbers, cavities, port connections and hose sizes for your settings.
Electronics (live)The camera, the ESP32 controller, the relays, the solenoids, the pump motor contactor and the E-stop.
Readouts, Results, Settings (Optional)Values now, totals of the runs, and all settings.

Choices in step 2

The effect of each choice
ChoiceEffect
In the side trayThe arm moves each watermelon to the side and puts it in a tray next to the arm.
In the deck binThe conveyor slides down. The arm puts the fruit on the belt, and the belt carries it into the bin on the deck (12 places for round fruit).
Clear leaves before each pickThe air knife in front of the arm blows the leaves away. If the first camera image is not good, a puff of air at the gripper clears the area. Then the camera looks a second time.
Basic: on/off valvesTwo valves in series move one cylinder at a time. The camera guides the arm.
UpgradedProportional valves in parallel move the two cylinders together on smooth straight paths. Position sensors on the cylinders guide the arm.
Wrist rotationA worm gearmotor turns the jaws, so that they close across the narrow side of an elongated watermelon. The gripper hangs 1.5 in lower. When the gripper holds the fruit, the wrist turns back to 0 degrees and the fruit turns with it. With the wrist fitted, the stowed conveyor moves 8 in farther away, so that the gripper does not hit it.
Find watermelons with the camera while drivingThe rover does not know where the fruit is. The camera searches ahead. Yellow rings show possible fruit. A ring becomes blue when the fruit is confirmed. The camera can miss some fruit, and leaf piles can cause false stops.

Demo scenes

Start each scene with a new field: push Remove all watermelons, then Plant. Speed 4x is satisfactory.

  1. Basic harvest. Round fruit, no leaves, deck bin. The full cycle: drive, find, pick, convey and put in the bin.
  2. Realistic canopy. Whole row at 80%, leaf clearing on, camera search while driving on. Look at the rings, the air knife, and the found, missed and false-stop counts in Results.
  3. Fruit shape. Elongated fruit, first with the wrist off, then on. Without the wrist, the arm does not pick many fruit, because they are too wide for the jaws.
  4. Smoother hardware. Basic, then Upgraded. Compare the movement and the valve openings in the live circuit.
  5. Use the rover for other tasks. Remove the harvest module and drive the rover without it.

Readouts and results

Readouts show the arm, the work zone, the gripper, the hydraulics, the camera, the canopy, the harvest, the power and battery, and the stability. Results keep totals: picks placed and tried, cycle time, grab error, gripper hits stopped, picks refused by the checks, energy for each pick, and picks for each battery charge.

In this twin, Show worst point searches the pick line only. The lab and tractor twin searches the full work path, from the pick line to the drop point.

The live hydraulic circuit

With Basic hardware, the circuit shows the on/off valves in series. With Upgraded hardware, it shows the parallel circuit: pressure compensators, proportional valves, load-sense shuttle valves, counterbalance valves, cylinders and string potentiometers. Open Parts, ports and hoses below the circuit for the part numbers, the cavities, the port connections and the hose sizes for your settings.
