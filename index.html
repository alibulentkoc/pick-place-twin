Hydraulic pick-and-place digital twin

Two cylinders hang from a bar and meet at a gripper. A depth camera finds the object, and the valves move the tip to pick it up. Everything runs in your browser, offline, from a single file.

Built for AGM 4060 Fluid Power Systems at Clemson University, and extended into the Amiga Hydraulic Arm, a harvester on a farm-ng Amiga rover.

Lab and tractor twin

The course rig on the Eaton HTS-2 trainer, and the design project: a harvesting arm on a tractor. Check the geometry, the bin clearance, the worst point of the work path, the cylinder forces and pressures, and the counterbalance valves. Manual, camera-guided and timed control, a live circuit, electronics and the camera pipeline.

The arm on a farm-ng Amiga rover that drives along a row. Leaf canopy and an air knife, finding fruit while driving, a conveyor to a deck bin, wrist rotation, upgraded proportional hydraulics and a removable harvest module.
