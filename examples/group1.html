How we chose the frame

We started from the tractor start values and changed one value at a time. Each card below is one trial. The five design values of the trial are in the gray boxes; the value that we changed in that trial is yellow. Under them are the three checks of the twin, and why the trial passed or failed. Select "Show in the twin" to load the trial into the twin and see the result yourself.

The rule behind every result. A cylinder can be no shorter than its retracted length L_min and no longer than L_min + S. The tip can reach a point only if both cylinder lengths to that point are in this range. Each length is L = √(Δx² + Δy²), from the pivot of the cylinder to the tip. The pivots are at x = -s/2 and x = +s/2, at the bar height H.

Trial 1. Tractor start

Pivot spacing s60 in
Bar height H72 in
Retracted length L_min40 in
Stroke S30 in
Bin inner rim distance x_b45 in
  • FAIL Pick width reached: only -10.6 to 10.6 in
  • FAIL Drop point: not reachable
  • - Rim clearance: not checked

Why: The pivots are at x = ±30 in. To reach the far pick point (-24, 15), cylinder 2 must be √(54² + 57²) = 78.5 in long: more than L_min + S = 70 in.

Decision: The pivots are too far apart. Make s smaller.

Trial 2. Smaller pivot spacing

Changed: s: 60 to 30 in

Pivot spacing s30 in
Bar height H72 in
Retracted length L_min40 in
Stroke S30 in
Bin inner rim distance x_b45 in
  • PASS Pick width reached: -25.6 to 25.6 in
  • FAIL Drop point: not reachable
  • - Rim clearance: not checked

Why: For the far pick point, cylinder 2 now needs √(39² + 57²) = 69.1 in: less than 70 in. For the drop point (53, 55), cylinder 1 needs √(68² + 17²) = 70.1 in: just more than 70 in.

Decision: The pick width passes, but the drop point is still out of reach. Make s smaller again.

Trial 3. Smaller pivot spacing again

Changed: s: 30 to 16 in

Pivot spacing s16 in
Bar height H72 in
Retracted length L_min40 in
Stroke S30 in
Bin inner rim distance x_b45 in
  • PASS Pick width reached: -32.6 to 32.6 in
  • PASS Drop point: reachable
  • FAIL Rim clearance: NOT OK with the bin at 45 in

Why: At the drop point, cylinder 1 needs 63.3 in and cylinder 2 needs 48.1 in: both in the range 40 to 70 in. But when the front of the fruit is above the rim, the tip must be at (42, 52). Cylinder 2 would need √(34² + 20²) = 39.4 in: shorter than L_min = 40 in. The cylinder cannot retract enough to lift the fruit that high there.

Decision: Both reach checks pass. Move the bin away from the arm.

Trial 4. Bin further out

Changed: x_b: 45 to 46.5 in (46.0 in still fails)

Pivot spacing s16 in
Bar height H72 in
Retracted length L_min40 in
Stroke S30 in
Bin inner rim distance x_b46.5 in
  • PASS Pick width reached: -32.6 to 32.6 in
  • PASS Drop point: reachable
  • PASS Rim clearance: OK at 46.5 in

Why: The rim point moves to (43.5, 52). Cylinder 2 now needs √(35.5² + 20²) = 40.7 in: longer than L_min = 40 in.

Decision: The first design that passes all checks. The forces at the worst point are 321.1 lb push (cylinder 1) and 329.9 lb pull (cylinder 2).

Trial 5. Catalog cylinder: our design

Changed: L_min: 40 to 40.25 in (Bailey 288411, 2 x 30 in)

Pivot spacing s16 in
Bar height H72 in
Retracted length L_min40.25 in
Stroke S30 in
Bin inner rim distance x_b46.5 in
  • PASS Pick width reached: -33.1 to 33.0 in
  • PASS Drop point: reachable
  • PASS Rim clearance: OK at 46.5 in

Why: The real cylinder is 0.25 in longer when retracted. The reach changes very little. The forces stay at 321.1 lb and 329.9 lb: they depend only on where the pivots and the tip are, not on the length range of the cylinders.

Decision: This is our design. Trials 6 and 7 check two other choices against it.

Trial 6. Check: a shorter cylinder

Decision: Smaller forces, but the pick width fails. Keep H = 72 in.

After trials 6 and 7, select "Load the Group 1 values again" to go back to the design.

Pick and place

Select the button: the twin puts three cantaloupes at random places in the pick width (at least 14 in apart) and moves each one to the bin. It shows the path: up from the fruit, along the top of the reach band, over the bin rim, and down to the drop point. Watch the status line in the twin for each step.

The demonstration moves the tip along a safe path. It does not show the valve timing. For the cycle time, use F17 and F18 (Cylinder card row 13): 6.46 s.

The twin also has its own automatic mode (panel step 4, Run the arm: Camera auto, then Pick all). That mode uses the camera and the valve control, as the real rig does. It is optional for your project. It can stop with a message such as "Hunting around the target"; the message tells you which setting to change.
