Point at a value that has a dotted line. The twin shows where it comes from: the settings field and the Readouts line flash in yellow. Select the value to move the tip to the point that the value is about.

This example shows the method and the format. Your crop, tractor and numbers are different. If you copy its numbers, your answers are wrong.

Figure 1 and the twin

Figure 1 shows the Group 1 arm, seen from behind the tractor. The twin starts in the same front view, with the same values. The dashed cylinders in Figure 1 show the tip at the far pick point; the solid cylinders show the tip at the drop point. Select y_g or y_d below to move the twin tip to these points. At y_d the cantaloupe is in the gripper, above the bin.

Three things are in the twin but not in Figure 1. The tractor and its 3-point hitch carry the frame; the frame is between the arm and the tractor, so the arm moves in front of it. The camera is on its mast above the bar. The shaded band shows all tip positions that the cylinders can reach; its color shows the cylinder force for each pound of load. The band stops above the ground because the fingers must stay above the ground.

Pick and place

Select the button: the twin puts three cantaloupes at random places in the pick width (at least 14 in apart) and moves each one to the bin. It shows the path: up from the fruit, along the top of the reach band, over the bin rim, and down to the drop point. Watch the status line in the twin for each step.

The demonstration moves the tip along a safe path. It does not show the valve timing. For the cycle time, use F17 and F18 (Cylinder card row 13): 6.46 s.
